# Phantom Cushion

Minecraft mod that adds the Phantom Cushion item, an invisible variant of the wool cushions.

[![modrinth](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@intergrav/devins-badges@3/assets/cozy/available/modrinth_vector.svg)](https://modrinth.com/mod/phantom-cushion)

> The Phantom Cushion is a craftable invisible cushion that works like a normal wool cushion, but disappears when placed.
> 
> ---
> 
> Phantom cushions have a single color. They become temporarily visible when looking directly at them, or while holding a phantom cushion in your hand.
> 
> ![Showcase GIF](./assets/showcase.gif)
> 
> ---
> 
> The phantom cushion can be crafted by combining any wool cushion with a phantom membrane. They can also be crafted back into normal cushions by combining them with wool.
> 
> ![Crafting recipes GIF](./assets/crafting.gif)
